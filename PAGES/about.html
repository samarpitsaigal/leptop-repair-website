OUR STORY

From a Small Bench to a Trusted Repair Hub

What started in 2016 as a single-bench repair desk in Delhi is today one of the most trusted laptop care centres in the city. We began with one simple belief — every customer deserves an honest opinion and a fair price.

Over the years we have expanded into a team of certified technicians who handle everything from minor keyboard swaps to complex chip-level motherboard repairs. But some things never changed: quality parts, transparent pricing and support long after the repair is done.
